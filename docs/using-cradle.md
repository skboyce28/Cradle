# Using Cradle

A tour of every screen, for the people doing the feeding. The same tips live in the app under the profile menu, then **Guide**.

**Finding your way around:**
- **Tabs:** Log, Dashboard, History, left to right.
- **Top right:** the 🍼 bottle button opens Night shift, the 🌙 moon button opens Calm, and the round profile button (with your picture) opens everything else.
- **Changing days:** use the date arrows under the title; **Today** jumps back.

---

## The triple-feed cycle

Each session has three parts: **nurse** at the breast, offer a **bottle** top-up, then **pump**. Log all three on **one row**. If you only nurse or only bottle feed, turn off what you don't use in **Settings**, then **What you track** (see [below](#what-you-track)).

### What you track

In **Settings**, **What you track** has switches for **🤱 Breast**, **🍼 Bottle**, and **⚡ Pump** (on to start), and **😴 Sleep** (off to start). With Sleep on, a **🦉 Dream Sock** box appears beside it for an [Owlet Dream Sock](owlet.md); turning Sleep on asks once whether you have one. Turn off what your family doesn't use and Cradle hides it everywhere:

| Off | What disappears |
|---|---|
| **Breast** | L and R, Minutes and the ▶ timer, the left and right split, weighted feeds; in Night shift, the timer and L/R (Start feed no longer starts a nursing timer); the side balance and Side starts on the Dashboard; nursing insights; the Breast and Min columns on the day sheet and Nursing on the doctor report |
| **Bottle** | The Bottle box on each feed and the Bottle tile; in Night shift, the bottle buttons; Bottle per day; bottle insights; the Bottle columns in print |
| **Pump** | The Pump box, the pump timer, and the Pumped tile; Pumped per day; pumping insights; the Pump columns in print |
| **Sleep** | [Sleep](#sleep): the 😴 Asleep button, the asleep or awake line, sleep rows, and the Sleep tile on the Log; the Night shift button; the sleep goal in Settings; the Dashboard tile, chart, filter, and insight; the Sleep column and tag in History, print, and the doctor report |

- **Daily volume chart and Bottle & pump filter:** these hide when both Bottle and Pump are off.
- **Shared with your family:** the choice syncs like the other Settings.
- **Nothing logged is deleted:** turning something back on brings it all back.
- **At least one of Breast, Bottle, and Pump stays on.**

## Log

Tap **+ Log feed now** when a feed starts. Each row has:

| Part | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Set to now; tap to change it. |
| **L / R** | Which breast. If you nurse on both sides, tap both. The first side you tap gets a dot, so you know where to start next time. |
| **Minutes** | How long you nursed. Tap ▶ when you start and ■ when you finish to fill it in, or type it. |
| **Left ↔ Right** | Appears when both sides are on. Slide it toward the side that got more time and it divides the minutes: 20 minutes slid to 75% left is 15 left and 5 right. The middle is an even split. Before minutes are entered it shows percentages. |
| **Bottle** | mL of the top-up. The bar fills toward your bottle target **per feed** (Settings). |
| **Pump** | mL pumped. |
| **Wet / Stool** | Mark both if a diaper had both. |
| **☀️ Vitamin D** | Tap on the feed where you gave the drops. Cradle warns you if it's already logged that day. |
| **⚖️ Weighted feed** | Enter the baby's weight before and after, in **grams** or in **lb** and **oz** (separate boxes, with the number keyboard). Tap **g** or **lb oz** to switch; what you've typed converts. Cradle shows the estimated transfer (1 gram is about 1 mL). |
| **Note** | Anything else. |

**🧷 Diaper only** adds a row for a change between feeds. Those rows don't count as feeds.

**The day's totals** sit above the list, in pairs on a phone: **Feeds** and **Vitamin D**, **Pumped** and **Bottle**, **Wet** and **Stool**, then **Bath**.

### Baths

**🛁 Bath** adds a bath with the current time. Each bath row has:

| Part | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Set to now; tap to change it. |
| **Sponge / Tub** | The kind of bath. Cradle starts the next one with whichever you used last. |
| **Note** | Hair wash, nail trim, anything else. |

Baths **never count as feeds**, so they don't change feed totals, the time between feeds, or when the next feed is due. The **Bath** tile under the day's totals shows today's bath, or when the last one was.

### Tummy time

**🤸 Tummy time** adds a tummy time row with the current time and starts its timer:

| Part | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Time** | When it started; tap to change it. |
| **Minutes** | Tap **■** when tummy time is over and the minutes are saved (to the nearest minute, at least 1). Or type them. **▶** times another stretch on the same row and adds it. |
| **Note** | On a play mat, on a chest, anything else. |

Only one tummy time timer runs at a time. On an earlier day there's no timer, so enter the time and minutes. Tummy time **never counts as a feed**. The **Tummy time** tile under the day's totals fills toward your daily goal (Settings, 15 minutes to start; 0 turns it off) and turns green when you reach it. Start with a few minutes a few times a day and build up; ask your pediatrician what fits your baby.

### Sleep

Turn on **😴 Sleep** in Settings, under What you track. It's off to start.

- **😴 Asleep**, under Since last feed, adds a sleep row with the current time. The button becomes **☀️ Woke up**; tap it (or **■ Woke up** on the row) when the baby wakes and the wake time and length are saved.
- **Everyone sees it:** a sleep in progress syncs like any entry, so another phone shows the baby asleep and can tap Woke up.
- **The line under Since last feed** shows **Asleep 1h 35m since 2:40pm**, or between sleeps the wake window: **Awake 1h 05m since 4:15pm**.
- **Typing times:** each row has a start and a wake time; change either and the length updates. On an earlier day, enter both.
- **Naps and night:** a sleep that starts between 7pm and 7am is night sleep (🌙); others are naps (😴).
- **Across midnight:** a sleep from 9pm to 2am counts 3 hours on the first day and 2 on the next.
- **Overlaps count once:** if two sleeps overlap (one tapped by hand and one from the Owlet sock, say), the totals merge them, so the time isn't counted twice.
- **Never a feed:** sleep doesn't change feed counts, gaps, or the next feed.
- **Owlet Dream Sock:** with Sleep on, connect the sock in Settings (**🦉 Owlet Dream Sock**; your family must require [two-step sign-in](accounts-and-families.md#two-step-sign-in)) and sleep is logged automatically, marked **from Owlet**. The line under Since last feed adds the sock's status, like **🦉 Asleep, checked just now**. See [Owlet Dream Sock](owlet.md).
- **The Sleep tile** fills toward your daily goal (Settings, 15 hours to start; 0 turns it off) and shows naps and night separately. Newborns often sleep 14 to 17 hours a day in short stretches; ask your pediatrician what fits your baby.

**Day notes** sit at the bottom of the day.

**Pump timer:** start it when you start pumping. The green arc shows your target window (15 to 20 minutes to start; change it in Settings).

### Next feed

The Log shows when the next feed is due: the last feed's time plus your feed interval (3 hours to start). When it's due, Cradle chimes, vibrates, and shows **Snooze 10 min** and **Dismiss**.

- **It only rings while Cradle is open on screen.** Tap the screen once after opening Cradle so your phone allows sound. Night shift and Calm keep the screen awake when Cradle is installed as an app.
- **On a phone, ⏰ Set alarm** shows the time to set in your Clock app, big and clear, with the steps (Open Clock, Tap +, Pick the time). Phones don't let web apps create Clock alarms themselves, even when Cradle is installed, so that one is a few quick taps.
- **Turn the in-app alarm off** in Settings.
- **📣 Notifications, even with Cradle closed:** in Settings, choose **Notify this phone**: when a feed is due, or 15 or 30 minutes before. Each phone chooses for itself. This needs a one-time setup by whoever runs your Cradle; see [Notifications](notifications.md). On iPhone, add Cradle to the Home Screen first.

### Diaper goals

Goals follow the baby's age, counting the birth day as **Day 0**:

| Day of life | Wet | Stool |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 (birth day) | 1 | 1 |
| Days 1 to 5 | same as the day (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) | same as the day |
| Day 6 on | at least 4 | at least 4 |

The day-6-on numbers are set in Settings. Follow whatever your care team tells you.

## Night shift

A dim, low-glare screen with huge buttons, for one-handed feeds in the dark. Open it with the 🍼 bottle button at the top right, next to the moon.

1. Tap **Start feed**. It logs the time and starts the timer.
2. Tap **L** or **R**. With both on, a slider splits the time between them, like on the Log.
3. Adjust the bottle in 5 mL steps.
4. Mark wet, stool, or Vitamin D.
5. Tap **Done**.

Between feeds, the start screen also has **🧷 Diaper only** and **🛁 Bath**, which log those with the current time, and, with Sleep turned on, **😴 Asleep** (it becomes **☀️ Woke up** with how long the baby has slept). Change a bath's kind or add a note on the Log afterward.

Everything saves as you go. The three dots at the top switch colors: **Warm**, **Moonlight**, or **Red** (easiest on night vision). ✕ closes it.

## Dashboard

The 7 or 14 days ending on the selected day:

- Feeds per day, nursing minutes, bottle and pump volume
- Diapers against goals, Vitamin D streak, side balance (which side you started on, and nursing time on each side from the split slider), longest stretch between feeds
- Baths: how many, sponge or tub, and when the last one was
- Tummy time: minutes per day, how many completed days met your goal, today so far, and a daily chart with the goal line
- Sleep (when turned on): hours per day over the days with sleep logged, how many met your goal, the longest stretch, today so far, and a daily chart of night sleep and naps with the goal line. With an [Owlet Dream Sock](owlet.md), the tile also gives how much came from Owlet and how much was logged by hand, and a thin teal line beside each bar shows the Owlet part
- Weight against birth weight: weigh-ins are filled dots joined by a line. A weighted feed shows its before weight as an open circle and its after weight (which includes the milk just taken) as a small dot above it, joined by a dashed line. Only before weights count toward Latest, Lowest, and the other weight numbers

**Filter chips** under the Print button narrow the Dashboard to one topic: **Feeds**, **Bottle & pump**, **Diapers**, **Vitamin D**, **Tummy time**, **Sleep** (when turned on), **Weight**, or **Baths**. Only that topic's cards and insights show; **All** brings everything back. Cradle remembers your choice on this phone, and **Print** prints what's showing.

**Tap any chart to enlarge it**, then tap a day or a feed for details. **Today counts as "in progress"** until midnight, so a goal that isn't met *yet* isn't shown as missed.

**+ Add weight** logs a weight from a visit, in grams or in lb and oz, the way the scale shows it. Tap **g** or **lb oz** to switch. In grams, a small number like `3.27` is read as kilograms.

**See all** (or **Weights** in the profile menu) lists every weight, newest first, above the form:
- **Edit** fills the form with that weight. Change it and tap **Save changes**, or **Cancel edit**.
- **×** deletes it.
- Weights from weighted feeds have **Open feed**, which jumps to that feed on the Log. The birth weight has **Settings**.

**🩺 Doctor report** prints one page for appointments:
- weight against birth weight
- daily feeds, nursing minutes with the left and right split, and bottle and pump volumes
- diapers against goals, Vitamin D, baths, and tummy time
- sleep per day and a Sleep column, when Sleep is turned on
- weighted feeds and your day notes
- lines for questions

## History

Every day at a glance, as cards or a table. A 🛁 tag or the Bath column shows each day's baths, a 🤸 tag or the Tummy column its tummy time, and a 😴 tag or the Sleep column its sleep (green when the goal was met).

## Calm

The 🌙 button at the top right. A slow, soothing screensaver with the time, time since the last feed, and the next feed due. **Tap the screen** to show controls:

- **Sounds:** spa music, ocean, rain, or white noise, with volume
- **Clock position:** center or corner

The feed alarm still works here.

## Milk storage

In the profile menu, after Weights. It keeps track of pumped milk in the fridge and freezer, so you know what you have and what to use first. Everyone in the family sees the same stash.

**Adding milk.** Fill in the form at the bottom as milk goes in:
- **Pumped:** the date and time it was pumped (now, to start with). For milk already in the freezer, use the date on the bag.
- **Amount:** type the number, and tap **mL** or **oz** next to the box. Your choice is remembered on that phone (it starts on oz when weights are in lb and oz).
- **Goes in:** Freezer or Fridge.
- **Container:** storage bag, bottle, tray or cubes, or other.
- **How many:** to add several containers of the same amount at once.
- **Label** and **Note:** optional. See labels below.

**The list.** Totals for the freezer, fridge, and any thawed milk come first, in mL and oz. Each section lists the oldest first, and the oldest frozen milk is marked **Use first**. Cradle follows the [CDC's storage guidelines](https://www.cdc.gov/breastfeeding/breast-milk-preparation-and-storage/handling-breastmilk.html):

| Where | Cradle shows | Amber (use soon) | Orange (past the limit) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fridge, fresh | Use or freeze by (4 days after pumping) | Under a day left | Over 4 days |
| Freezer | Age and best-by date (6 months) | Past 6 months | Past 12 months |
| Thawed, in the fridge | Use by (24 hours after thawing) | Under 6 hours left | Over 24 hours |

These are guidelines, not medical advice; ask your care team if you're unsure.

**Buttons on each container:**
- **Freeze** (fridge): moves it to the freezer. Not offered once it's over 4 days old.
- **Thaw** (freezer): moves it to the fridge and starts the 24 hours. Thawed milk can't go back in the freezer.
- **Use** (fridge or thawed): **Log a bottle feed now** adds a bottle feed with that amount to today and shows it on the Log; or **Used, don't log a feed**; or **Throw it away**.
- **Edit:** change it, print its label, or **Delete** a mistake.

**Recently used** shows the last 10 containers used or thrown away, with **Undo** (a bottle feed it logged stays in the Log).

**Labels** are optional. Three ways to use them:
- **Your own labels:** type the label or code into **Label**.
- **Scanning:** tap 📷 to scan a barcode or QR code with the camera. This works where the browser has a built-in barcode reader, like Chrome on Android; elsewhere (Safari and other iPhone browsers, for example), the 📷 buttons are hidden and you type the label. A Bluetooth or USB barcode scanner works anywhere, because it types the code for you.
- **Cradle's labels:** **Print labels** gives each container without a label a code like `CM-7KQ2M`, then asks which kind:
  - **Label printer, 40 × 14 mm** (the NIIMBOT P21 and similar): Cradle makes a picture of each label (the baby's name and amount, a Code 128 barcode, and the code with the date pumped). Tap **Share or save** to save them to your photos, or **Download** one. In the printer's app, start a 40 × 14 mm label, add a picture, pick the label, and print it at full size.
  - **Sheet of labels** for a regular printer, three across, with the same details. Use sticker paper or tape them on.

  **Edit**, then **Print label**, makes one again. Cradle remembers which kind you picked.
- **Labels with a date:** a label written like `10/10/2026 - 8 A` or `10/9/26 7:30 PM` (typed, scanned, or found) fills in the date and time pumped. Containers pumped together can share a label; saving a label that's already in storage asks first.

**Find by label** at the top filters the list as you type. Press Enter, scan with **📷 Scan**, or use a Bluetooth scanner to jump to that container. A label Cradle hasn't seen starts a new container with it.

## Learning center

In the profile menu. **High-contrast cards** are five original black-and-white designs: Bullseye, Checkerboard, Zigzag, Spiral, and Smile. Bold contrast is among the first things newborns can make out, so they're something to share during calm, awake moments.

- Tap a card to show it full screen. Swipe, or tap the left or right side, to change cards.
- **Invert** swaps black and white. **Auto** changes cards every 10 seconds.
- Hold the phone about 8 to 12 inches (20 to 30 cm) from your baby's face. A few minutes is plenty; stop when your baby looks away, yawns, or fusses.
- The screen stays on while a card is showing. ✕ or the back gesture returns to the Learning center.

## Fun center

In the profile menu: three games for the in-between moments, with shared high scores.

### Bottle Catch

A two-minute, one-thumb arcade game.

| Falls | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🍼 Bottle | +10. Catching in a row builds a multiplier, up to ×4 after 15 in a row. Missing one resets it. |
| 🍼 Golden bottle (glowing) | +50, times the multiplier. Rare and quick. |
| 🧷 Safety pin | Costs a heart. You have 3; losing all 3 ends the round early. |
| 🧸 Pacifier | Slows everything down for 5 seconds. |

- **Controls:** drag anywhere to move the basket. On a computer, the arrow keys work too, and Space pauses.
- **Pace:** things fall faster as the 2 minutes run down.
- **Pausing:** the game pauses by itself when you switch apps or the feed alarm rings. **❚❚** pauses it by hand; **End round** finishes early and keeps your score.
- **Sound and buzz:** sound starts **off** so it won't wake anyone. **Buzz** (a light vibration) and **Sound** switch on the start and pause screens.

### Daily Word

A new word every day at midnight. Guess it in 6 tries.

- **Pick 4, 5, or 6 letters** on the start screen (the **?** button). Everyone who picks the same length gets the same word that day, so the family can compare. Once you've made today's first guess, a new choice applies from tomorrow (and to practice).
- **Colors:**

  | Color | Means |
  |---|---|
  | 🟩 Green | Right letter, right spot |
  | 🟨 Yellow | In the word, wrong spot |
  | ⬛ Gray | Not in the word |

  A letter you typed twice only turns yellow as many times as it's in the word. The keyboard shows what you've found. **⌫** (delete) is at the left of the bottom row and **Enter** at the right.
- **Guesses must be real words.** You can type about 3,100 four-letter, 6,400 five-letter, and 10,700 six-letter words. The answers come from a smaller list of common words (about 1,400, 1,900, and 2,400), with no plurals, past tenses, or anything rude or grim.
- **Hard mode:** green letters must stay in their spot and yellow letters must be used in every later guess. Worth 10 extra points. Choose it before today's first guess.
- **Points:** 70 for solving in 1 guess, 60 in 2, down to 20 in 6, and 0 if you don't get it. +10 in hard mode.
- **Your stats:** played, win %, current streak, best streak, and how many guesses your wins took. A streak is days in a row solved; a lost or skipped day starts it over.
- **Share** copies your colored squares (never the letters) to paste into a text.
- **Practice:** unlimited random words at your length. No points, no stats, and nothing is posted.
- **Stop anytime.** Your guesses are saved on the phone, so a feed in the middle of a word is no problem.
- **One result per day per account.** If you already played today's word on another phone, this one shows your result (as colors) instead of a new game.
- **On a computer,** type with the keyboard: letters, Enter, and Backspace.

### Stork Flight

Tap to flap. Fly a stork carrying a bundle through the gaps between the clouds and the chimneys of a sleeping town.

| Thing | Points |
|---|---|
| Each gap you fly through | +10 |
| ⭐ Star | +5 |
| 🏠 A house with a lit window (a delivery) | +25 more |

- **Controls:** tap anywhere to flap; let go to glide down. On a computer, Space or the up arrow flaps and P pauses.
- **Starting:** the stork hovers until your first tap, so you can get ready.
- **Pace:** the first few gaps are wide and centered. After that the gaps move around, get narrower, and come faster.
- **Ending:** one bump into a cloud, a house, or the rooftops ends the flight. **❚❚** pauses; **End flight** finishes early and keeps your score.
- **Pausing, sound, and buzz** work like Bottle Catch: it pauses itself when you switch apps or the feed alarm rings, and sound starts off.

### High scores

Each game has its own boards; the buttons at the top of the High scores screen switch between them.

- **Initials:** your first posted score asks for **3 initials**. All three games use the same ones. They're remembered for your account, and **Change** on the board updates them, past scores included.
- **Bottle Catch and Stork Flight boards:**
  - **Everyone:** each player's best score across this Cradle site, top 10.
  - **Our family:** your family's top 10.
  - **My best:** your own top 10, with your rank.
- **Daily Word boards:**
  - **Everyone:** total points across this Cradle site, top 10, with each player's 🔥 streak and words solved.
  - **Our family:** your family's totals, plus each person's grid for today. Grids show colors only, and stay hidden until you've finished today's word, so nothing is spoiled.
  - **My days:** your last 10 days, with your grids.
- **What's shown:** only initials, scores, and colored grids, never names, emails, or letters.
- **Offline:** a Bottle Catch round or Stork Flight played offline is saved on the phone and posts when you're back online (if several are waiting, your best one posts). A Daily Word result posts when you're back online too, as long as that's within about a day.

### Support

Tap **💬 Support** in the profile menu to tell whoever runs your Cradle about a problem, an idea, or a question. You need to be signed in.

- **Kind:** **Bug or problem**, **Idea or request**, or **Question**. The box's hint changes to match (for a bug: what happened, and what you expected).
- **Screenshot (optional):** **Add a screenshot** shrinks it on your phone (to at most 1280 pixels, as a JPEG) before it's sent. **Remove** takes it off.
- **Sent with it:** your Cradle version, phone type and browser, whether it's installed, the screen size, and your account and family, so the problem can be reproduced. The form shows exactly what's included.
- **Your feedback** lists what you've sent with its status and any reply:

  | Status | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | **New** | Not looked at yet |
  | **Reviewed** | Read and considered |
  | **Planned** | Going to be done |
  | **Used** | Done, or the answer was given |
  | **Won't do** | Read, but not planned |

- **Achievements:** feedback marked anything other than New counts as reviewed (Won't do counts too). The first earns **Quality Assurance**; five earn **Compliance Officer**.
- Up to 10 a day per account.

**Admins** (see [Making someone an admin](deployment.md#support-admins)) also see a **Feedback inbox** button with the number of new ones. The inbox shows each item with who sent it, their family, version, and device; filter by status, load the screenshot, type a reply, and tap a status to save both. Two-step sign-in is required for admins who have it on.

### Your picture

Tap **Your picture** at the top of the profile menu.

- **Choose a picture:** upload a photo, keep the **Letter** (the baby's first initial, as before), or pick a built-in one: Dad, Mom, Person, Bear, Bunny, Fox, or Owl.
- **Photos** are cropped to the middle square and shrunk on your phone (to about 5 KB) before they're saved. Tap **Photo** again to choose a different one.
- **Who can see it:**

  | Choice | Who sees your picture |
  |---|---|
  | **Only me** | Just you, on your own phones. Everyone else sees the letter. |
  | **Me and my family** (to start) | Everyone who shares your log, on the Family screen |
  | **Everyone on this Cradle** | Also everyone else who uses the same Cradle site, next to your initials on the high-score boards |

- **Where it shows:** your profile button at the top right, the Family screen, and (if you chose Everyone) the high-score boards.
- **Removing a photo:** choose **Letter** or a built-in picture and Save. The photo is deleted from the database.
- Signed out, the picture is saved on this phone only.

| **Import data** | **Choose a file**, or paste a `CRADLE1:` code. **Merge** adds to your log without removing anything; **Replace** clears your log first. |
| **Print this tab** | Prints the current tab (or saves it as a PDF). The Log prints a paper sheet for the selected day, baths, tummy time, and sleep included. |
| **Erase all data** | Removes every entry from your family's log **on every phone**. Only the **family owner** can use it; for others it says *Only the family owner can do this*. Everyone can still delete single entries with ×. Export first if you want a copy. |
| **Sign out and remove from this phone** | Signs you out and clears the log, settings, and game scores from this phone, for a shared or lost phone. It syncs first and warns you if any changes haven't reached your account. Everything comes back when you sign in again. |

## Printing

Every tab has a **Print** button. Printing needs Cradle open in a real browser (Chrome, Safari) or as the installed app, not inside a file preview.

## Offline

Each phone keeps its own copy of the log. With no connection, keep logging as usual. The cloud icon in the header shows ⚠︎, and everything uploads when the phone reconnects.
