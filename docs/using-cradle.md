# Using Cradle

A tour of every screen, for the people doing the feeding. The same tips live in the app under the profile menu, then **Guide**.

**Finding your way around:**
- **Tabs:** Log, Night shift, Dashboard, History, left to right.
- **Top right:** the 🌙 moon button opens Calm; the round profile button opens everything else.
- **Changing days:** use the date arrows under the title; **Today** jumps back.

---

## The triple-feed cycle

Each session has three parts: **nurse** at the breast, offer a **bottle** top-up, then **pump**. Log all three on **one row**. If you only nurse or only bottle feed, turn off what you don't use in **Settings**, then **What you track** (see [below](#what-you-track)).

### What you track

In **Settings**, **What you track** has three switches: **🤱 Breast**, **🍼 Bottle**, and **⚡ Pump**. Turn off what your family doesn't use and Cradle hides it everywhere:

| Off | What disappears |
|---|---|
| **Breast** | L and R, Minutes and the ▶ timer, the left and right split, weighted feeds; in Night shift, the timer and L/R (Start feed no longer starts a nursing timer); the side balance and Side starts on the Dashboard; nursing insights; the Breast and Min columns on the day sheet and Nursing on the doctor report |
| **Bottle** | The Bottle box on each feed and the Bottle tile; in Night shift, the bottle buttons; Bottle per day; bottle insights; the Bottle columns in print |
| **Pump** | The Pump box, the pump timer, and the Pumped tile; Pumped per day; pumping insights; the Pump columns in print |

- **Daily volume chart and Bottle & pump filter:** these hide when both Bottle and Pump are off.
- **Shared with your family:** the choice syncs like the other Settings.
- **Nothing logged is deleted:** turning something back on brings it all back.
- **At least one stays on.**

## Log

Tap **+ Log feed now** when a feed starts. Each row has:

| Part | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Set to now; tap to change it. |
| **L / R** | Which breast. If you nurse on both sides, tap both. The first side you tap gets a dot, so you know where to start next time. |
| **Minutes** | How long you nursed. Tap ▶ when you start and ■ when you finish to fill it in, or type it. |
| **Left ↔ Right** | Appears when both sides are on. Slide it toward the side that got more time and it divides the minutes: 20 minutes slid to 75% left is 15 left and 5 right. The middle is an even split. Before minutes are entered it shows percentages. |
| **Bottle** | mL of the top-up. The bar fills toward your bottle target (Settings). |
| **Pump** | mL pumped. |
| **Wet / Stool** | Mark both if a diaper had both. |
| **☀️ Vitamin D** | Tap on the feed where you gave the drops. Cradle warns you if it's already logged that day. |
| **⚖️ Weighted feed** | Enter the baby's weight before and after. Cradle shows the estimated transfer (1 gram is about 1 mL). |
| **Note** | Anything else. |

**🧷 Diaper only** adds a row for a change between feeds. Those rows don't count as feeds.

### Baths

**🛁 Bath** adds a bath with the current time. Each bath row has:

| Part | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Set to now; tap to change it. |
| **Sponge / Tub** | The kind of bath. Cradle starts the next one with whichever you used last. |
| **Note** | Hair wash, nail trim, anything else. |

Baths **never count as feeds**, so they don't change feed totals, the time between feeds, or when the next feed is due. The **Bath** tile under the day's totals shows today's bath, or when the last one was.

**Day notes** sit at the bottom of the day.

**Pump timer:** start it when you start pumping. The green arc shows your target window (15 to 20 minutes to start; change it in Settings).

### Next feed

The Log shows when the next feed is due: the last feed's time plus your feed interval (3 hours to start). When it's due, Cradle chimes, vibrates, and shows **Snooze 10 min** and **Dismiss**.

- **It only rings while Cradle is open on screen.** Tap the screen once after opening Cradle so your phone allows sound. Night shift and Calm keep the screen awake when Cradle is installed as an app.
- **On a phone, ⏰ Set alarm** shows the time to set in your Clock app, big and clear, with the steps (Open Clock, Tap +, Pick the time). Phones don't let web apps create Clock alarms themselves, even when Cradle is installed, so that one is a few quick taps.
- **Turn the in-app alarm off** in Settings.
- **📣 Notifications, even with Cradle closed:** in Settings, choose **Notify this phone**: when a feed is due, or 15 or 30 minutes before. Each phone chooses for itself. This needs a one-time setup by whoever runs your Cradle; see [Notifications](notifications.md). On iPhone, add Cradle to the Home Screen first.

### Diaper goals

Goals follow the baby's age, counting the birth day as **Day 0**:

| Day of life | Wet | Stool |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 (birth day) | 1 | 1 |
| Days 1 to 5 | same as the day (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) | same as the day |
| Day 6 on | at least 4 | at least 4 |

The day-6-on numbers are set in Settings. Follow whatever your care team tells you.

## Night shift

A dim, low-glare screen with huge buttons, for one-handed feeds in the dark.

1. Tap **Start feed**. It logs the time and starts the timer.
2. Tap **L** or **R**. With both on, a slider splits the time between them, like on the Log.
3. Adjust the bottle in 5 mL steps.
4. Mark wet, stool, or Vitamin D.
5. Tap **Done**.

Between feeds, the start screen also has **🧷 Diaper only** and **🛁 Bath**, which log those with the current time. Change a bath's kind or add a note on the Log afterward.

Everything saves as you go. The three dots at the top switch colors: **Warm**, **Moonlight**, or **Red** (easiest on night vision). ✕ closes it.

## Dashboard

The 7 or 14 days ending on the selected day:

- Feeds per day, nursing minutes, bottle and pump volume
- Diapers against goals, Vitamin D streak, side balance (which side you started on, and nursing time on each side from the split slider), longest stretch between feeds
- Baths: how many, sponge or tub, and when the last one was
- Weight against birth weight

**Filter chips** under the Print button narrow the Dashboard to one topic: **Feeds**, **Bottle & pump**, **Diapers**, **Vitamin D**, **Weight**, or **Baths**. Only that topic's cards and insights show; **All** brings everything back. Cradle remembers your choice on this phone, and **Print** prints what's showing.

**Tap any chart to enlarge it**, then tap a day or a feed for details. **Today counts as "in progress"** until midnight, so a goal that isn't met *yet* isn't shown as missed.

**+ Add weight** logs a weight from a visit. Type it like `7 lb 3.5 oz` or `3270 g`.

**See all** (or **Weights** in the profile menu) lists every weight, newest first, above the form:
- **Edit** fills the form with that weight. Change it and tap **Save changes**, or **Cancel edit**.
- **×** deletes it.
- Weights from weighted feeds have **Open feed**, which jumps to that feed on the Log. The birth weight has **Settings**.

**🩺 Doctor report** prints one page for appointments:
- weight against birth weight
- daily feeds, nursing minutes with the left and right split, and bottle and pump volumes
- diapers against goals, Vitamin D, and baths
- weighted feeds and your day notes
- lines for questions

## History

Every day at a glance, as cards or a table. A 🛁 tag or the Bath column shows each day's baths.

## Calm

The 🌙 button at the top right. A slow, soothing screensaver with the time, time since the last feed, and the next feed due. **Tap the screen** to show controls:

- **Sounds:** spa music, ocean, rain, or white noise, with volume
- **Clock position:** center or corner

The feed alarm still works here.

## Learning center

In the profile menu. **High-contrast cards** are five original black-and-white designs: Bullseye, Checkerboard, Zigzag, Spiral, and Smile. Bold contrast is among the first things newborns can make out, so they're something to share during calm, awake moments.

- Tap a card to show it full screen. Swipe, or tap the left or right side, to change cards.
- **Invert** swaps black and white. **Auto** changes cards every 10 seconds.
- Hold the phone about 8 to 12 inches (20 to 30 cm) from your baby's face. A few minutes is plenty; stop when your baby looks away, yawns, or fusses.
- The screen stays on while a card is showing. ✕ or the back gesture returns to the Learning center.

## Fun center

In the profile menu: three games for the in-between moments, with shared high scores.

### Bottle Catch

A two-minute, one-thumb arcade game.

| Falls | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🍼 Bottle | +10. Catching in a row builds a multiplier, up to ×4 after 15 in a row. Missing one resets it. |
| 🍼 Golden bottle (glowing) | +50, times the multiplier. Rare and quick. |
| 🧷 Safety pin | Costs a heart. You have 3; losing all 3 ends the round early. |
| 🧸 Pacifier | Slows everything down for 5 seconds. |

- **Controls:** drag anywhere to move the basket. On a computer, the arrow keys work too, and Space pauses.
- **Pace:** things fall faster as the 2 minutes run down.
- **Pausing:** the game pauses by itself when you switch apps or the feed alarm rings. **❚❚** pauses it by hand; **End round** finishes early and keeps your score.
- **Sound and buzz:** sound starts **off** so it won't wake anyone. **Buzz** (a light vibration) and **Sound** switch on the start and pause screens.

### Daily Word

A new word every day at midnight. Guess it in 6 tries.

- **Pick 4, 5, or 6 letters** on the start screen (the **?** button). Everyone who picks the same length gets the same word that day, so the family can compare. Once you've made today's first guess, a new choice applies from tomorrow (and to practice).
- **Colors:**

  | Color | Means |
  |---|---|
  | 🟩 Green | Right letter, right spot |
  | 🟨 Yellow | In the word, wrong spot |
  | ⬛ Gray | Not in the word |

  A letter you typed twice only turns yellow as many times as it's in the word. The keyboard shows what you've found.
- **Guesses must be real words.** You can type about 3,100 four-letter, 6,400 five-letter, and 10,700 six-letter words. The answers come from a smaller list of common words (about 1,400, 1,900, and 2,400), with no plurals, past tenses, or anything rude or grim.
- **Hard mode:** green letters must stay in their spot and yellow letters must be used in every later guess. Worth 10 extra points. Choose it before today's first guess.
- **Points:** 70 for solving in 1 guess, 60 in 2, down to 20 in 6, and 0 if you don't get it. +10 in hard mode.
- **Your stats:** played, win %, current streak, best streak, and how many guesses your wins took. A streak is days in a row solved; a lost or skipped day starts it over.
- **Share** copies your colored squares (never the letters) to paste into a text.
- **Practice:** unlimited random words at your length. No points, no stats, and nothing is posted.
- **Stop anytime.** Your guesses are saved on the phone, so a feed in the middle of a word is no problem.
- **One result per day per account.** If you already played today's word on another phone, this one shows your result (as colors) instead of a new game.
- **On a computer,** type with the keyboard: letters, Enter, and Backspace.

### Stork Flight

Tap to flap. Fly a stork carrying a bundle through the gaps between the clouds and the chimneys of a sleeping town.

| Thing | Points |
|---|---|
| Each gap you fly through | +10 |
| ⭐ Star | +5 |
| 🏠 A house with a lit window (a delivery) | +25 more |

- **Controls:** tap anywhere to flap; let go to glide down. On a computer, Space or the up arrow flaps and P pauses.
- **Starting:** the stork hovers until your first tap, so you can get ready.
- **Pace:** the first few gaps are wide and centered. After that the gaps move around, get narrower, and come faster.
- **Ending:** one bump into a cloud, a house, or the rooftops ends the flight. **❚❚** pauses; **End flight** finishes early and keeps your score.
- **Pausing, sound, and buzz** work like Bottle Catch: it pauses itself when you switch apps or the feed alarm rings, and sound starts off.

### High scores

Each game has its own boards; the buttons at the top of the High scores screen switch between them.

- **Initials:** your first posted score asks for **3 initials**. All three games use the same ones. They're remembered for your account, and **Change** on the board updates them, past scores included.
- **Bottle Catch and Stork Flight boards:**
  - **Everyone:** each player's best score across this Cradle site, top 10.
  - **Our family:** your family's top 10.
  - **My best:** your own top 10, with your rank.
- **Daily Word boards:**
  - **Everyone:** total points across this Cradle site, top 10, with each player's 🔥 streak and words solved.
  - **Our family:** your family's totals, plus each person's grid for today. Grids show colors only, and stay hidden until you've finished today's word, so nothing is spoiled.
  - **My days:** your last 10 days, with your grids.
- **What's shown:** only initials, scores, and colored grids, never names, emails, or letters.
- **Offline:** a Bottle Catch round or Stork Flight played offline is saved on the phone and posts when you're back online (if several are waiting, your best one posts). A Daily Word result posts when you're back online too, as long as that's within about a day.

### Data management

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Export data** | **Everything** or **Last 2 days**, then: **Text message** (opens Messages with a short code starting `CRADLE1:`), **Share file**, **Copy code**, or **Download file** (a full backup, `cradle-YYYY-MM-DD.json`). |
| **Import data** | **Choose a file**, or paste a `CRADLE1:` code. **Merge** adds to your log without removing anything; **Replace** clears your log first. |
| **Print this tab** | Prints the current tab (or saves it as a PDF). The Log prints a paper sheet for the selected day, baths included. |
| **Erase all data** | Removes every entry from your family's log **on every phone**. Only the **family owner** can use it; for others it says *Only the family owner can do this*. Everyone can still delete single entries with ×. Export first if you want a copy. |
| **Sign out and remove from this phone** | Signs you out and clears the log, settings, and game scores from this phone, for a shared or lost phone. It syncs first and warns you if any changes haven't reached your account. Everything comes back when you sign in again. |

## Printing

Every tab has a **Print** button. Printing needs Cradle open in a real browser (Chrome, Safari) or as the installed app, not inside a file preview.

## Offline

Each phone keeps its own copy of the log. With no connection, keep logging as usual. The cloud icon in the header shows ⚠︎, and everything uploads when the phone reconnects.
