# Cradle Terms and Privacy

Version 2026-10-15

## The short version

- Cradle is a notebook for your baby's care. It is not medical advice and not a monitor.
- Your family's log is stored in this Cradle's database, and the person who runs this Cradle can see it.
- Cradle has no ads, no analytics, and no trackers, and your data is never sold.
- You can export your log or erase it at any time.

## Using Cradle

- **Not medical advice.** Goals and insights in Cradle are placeholders and simple arithmetic on what you log. Follow your pediatrician or care team, and call them, or emergency services, if you're worried about your baby. Cradle is not for emergencies.
- **What you log is yours to share.** Only invite people you want to see your family's log, and log only what you're comfortable keeping here.
- **Keep your account safe.** Use a password you don't use elsewhere, and turn on two-step sign-in. You're responsible for what happens with your account.
- **Built with AI, provided as is.** Cradle was built with help from Claude, an AI model by Anthropic. It has been tested but not independently audited. It's free, comes without any warranty, and may change or stop. Keep your own backup with Export data.

## Your data

- **What's stored:** your account (username or email; passwords are handled by Supabase's sign-in service and never stored by Cradle), your family membership, everything you log, your settings, your profile picture if you add one, Fun center scores with your initials, feedback you send through Support (with any screenshot, your Cradle version, and your phone type), and the record that you accepted these terms.
- **Where it's stored:** in this Cradle's database (a Supabase project run by the person who runs this Cradle) and on each phone where you sign in.
- **Who can see it:** the people in your family; the person who runs this Cradle, who has access to the whole database; everyone on this Cradle can see your initials and scores on the high-score boards; your profile picture follows the choice you make; Support feedback goes to this Cradle's admins.
- **Notifications (optional):** a short, encrypted "feed due" message passes through Apple's, Google's, or Mozilla's push service, which can't read it.
- **Owlet Dream Sock (optional):** your Owlet password is used once to connect and is never stored. Cradle keeps Owlet's sign-in, encrypted, and reads only the sock's sleep state, charging, and battery; heart rate and oxygen are never stored. The connection is unofficial, may stop working, and is only available in families that require two-step sign-in.
- **Your choices:** export your log at any time (profile menu, then Data management, then Export data). The family owner can erase the family's log. To delete your account, ask the person who runs this Cradle through Support.

## Changes to these terms

When these terms change, Cradle asks you to read and accept the new version before your log syncs again. Cradle records which version you accepted and when.
