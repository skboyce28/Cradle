    Guide

    🔁

    The triple-feed cycle

    Each session has three parts: nurse at the breast, offer a bottle top-up, then pump. Log all three on one row. Only nursing, or only bottles? In Settings, under What you track, turn off what you don’t use and Cradle hides it everywhere.

    🤱

    Breast tracking

    If you nurse on both sides, tap both. The first side you tap gets the dot, so you know where to start next time. To time a feed, tap ▶ next to Minutes when you start and ■ when you finish. The minutes fill in for you, or you can type them. With both sides on, a Left ↔ Right slider appears: slide it toward the side that got more time, and it splits the minutes (for example, 20 minutes slid to 75% left is 15 left and 5 right). Leave it in the middle for an even split.

    💧

    Diaper checks

    If a diaper has both urine and stool, mark both on that feed. For a change between feeds, tap 🧷 Diaper only; those rows don't count as feeds. Goals follow the baby's age: 1 of each on the birth day (Day 0), then 1 on day 1, up to 5 on day 5, then at least 4 of each per day.

    🛁

    Baths

    Tap 🛁 Bath under Since last feed to log a bath with the current time. Choose Sponge or Tub and add a note if you like, such as a hair wash or nail trim. Cradle remembers which kind you used last. Baths don’t count as feeds, so they never change feed totals or when the next feed is due. The Log shows today’s bath or when the last one was, and the Dashboard, History, print sheet, and doctor report all include baths.

    ☀️

    Vitamin D

    Tap ☀️ on the feed where you gave the drops. Giving them at the same feed each day, like the first morning feed, makes it easier to remember. You'll get a heads-up if it's already logged that day.

    ⏱️

    Pump timer

    Start the timer when you start pumping. The green arc shows your target window.

    📊

    Dashboard

    Shows the last 7 or 14 days ending on the day you have selected, including how many baths and when the last one was. Tap a chip under Print (Feeds, Bottle & pump, Diapers, Vitamin D, Weight, Baths) to see just that topic, or All for everything. Print prints what’s showing. Tap any chart to enlarge it, then tap a day or feed for details. The side balance also shows nursing time on each side, from the minutes and the split slider. Today counts as in progress until midnight.

    💬

    Send updates by text

    Each phone keeps its own copy of the log. To catch the other person up:

    1. On your phone: tap the profile button, then Data management, then Export data.
    2. Pick Last 2 days for a quick shift handoff, or Everything for a full copy.
    3. Tap 💬 Text message. Your messages app opens with the update ready. Send it.
    4. On their phone: long-press the message and tap Copy.
    5. Open Cradle, tap the profile button, then Data management, then Import data. Paste into the box and tap Continue.
    6. Tap Merge. New entries are added and matching times are updated. Nothing already logged is removed.

    If the text button doesn’t open your messages app, use 📋 Copy code and paste it into any message instead. Paste the whole message, including the line that starts with CRADLE.

    ☁︎

    Sign-in and sharing

    Sign in with your Cradle username (or email) and password. Everyone in your family shares one log: feeds, diapers, baths, notes, weights, and goals sync automatically, usually within about 15 seconds. Each phone also keeps its own copy, so Cradle works offline and catches up when it reconnects. The cloud icon in the header shows the sync status. In the profile menu, Sign out keeps the log on this phone for next time, and Sign out and remove from this phone (under Data management) clears the log, settings, and game scores from the phone too (it syncs first, and warns you if anything hasn’t reached your account yet). Either way, your log stays saved in your account.

    👪

    Family

    Everyone has their own login and a family shares one log. New here? On the sign-in screen tap Create an account, pick a username (or use your email) and a password. To share someone’s log, enter their share key (like K7QM-4XPD); leave it blank to start your own. Your share key is under Family in the profile menu, with a Share button that writes the invite text for you. The family owner can make a New key at any time, which stops the old one working, and can remove people. Members can leave. Other families never see your log. Everything saves as you type, with no Save button. If two of you edit the same feed, changes to different boxes are combined; if you both change the same box, the last one wins. When someone else’s change arrives, a note says who made it and the row flashes.

    💾

    Backups and files

    Your log is saved in your account and on each signed-in phone. Data management in the profile menu holds your data tools. Export data saves a backup file you can keep, or sends a short code by text message. Import data brings a file or code back: Merge adds to your log, Replace clears it first. Erase all data removes every entry from your family’s log on every phone, so only the family owner can use it; export first if you want a copy. Everyone can still delete single entries with ×. If a phone already has a log when you reach the sign-in screen, Cradle offers to download a backup before you sign in.

    🖨️

    Printing

    Every tab has a Print button. The Log tab prints a paper sheet for the selected day. Printing needs Cradle open in a browser like Chrome or Safari, not in a file preview.

    ⏰

    Next feed and alarm

    The Log tab shows when the next feed is due, based on the last feed and the interval in Settings (3 hours to start). When it's due, Cradle chimes, vibrates, and shows Snooze and Dismiss. This only works while Cradle is open on screen, so tap the screen once after opening it to allow sound. On a phone, ⏰ Set alarm shows the time to set in your Clock app, with the steps. Phones don’t let web apps add Clock alarms themselves, even when Cradle is installed, so that one takes a few taps. To be told when Cradle is closed, turn on 📣 Notify this phone in Settings: when a feed is due, or 15 or 30 minutes before. Each phone chooses for itself. On iPhone, add Cradle to the Home Screen first.

    ⚖️

    Weighted feeds and weight

    For a weighted feed, tap ⚖️ on the feed and enter the weight before and after. Cradle shows about how much was transferred (1 gram is about 1 mL). Log weights from visits with + Add weight on the Dashboard or Weights in the profile menu. Type weights like 7 lb 3.5 oz or 3270 g. Every weight is listed at the top of the Weights screen (See all on the Dashboard): tap Edit to fix one, or × to delete it. Weights from weighted feeds have Open feed, and the birth weight has Settings, where you change them.

    🩺

    Doctor report

    On the Dashboard, 🩺 Doctor report prints one page for appointments: weight against birth weight, daily feeds, nursing minutes with the left and right split, bottle and pump volumes, diapers against goals, Vitamin D, baths, weighted feeds, your day notes, and lines for questions. It covers the 7 or 14 days shown on the Dashboard.

    🌙

    Night shift

    Tap the Night shift tab for a dim, low-glare screen with huge buttons for one-handed night feeds. The three dots at the top switch colors: Warm, Moonlight, or Red. Start feed logs the time and starts the timer. Then tap L or R (with both on, a slider splits the time between sides), adjust the bottle by 5 mL, mark wet, stool, or Vitamin D, and tap Done. Everything saves as you go. Between feeds, 🧷 Diaper only and 🛁 Bath log those with the current time.

    🌌

    Calm screen

    A slow, soothing screensaver with the time, time since the last feed, and the next feed due. Open it with the moon button at the top right, next to your profile. Tap the screen to show controls: spa music, ocean, rain, or white noise, volume, and whether the clock sits in the center or the corner. The feed alarm still works here. When Cradle is installed as an app, the screen stays on while Calm or Night shift is open. Opened as a file, your phone may still turn the screen off on its own timer.

    🏆

    Achievements

    Fifty-three badges in eight groups (Feeds, Nursing, Bottle and pump, Diapers, Vitamin D, Baths, Weight and milestones, and Fun center), like Sunshine Streak for 7 days of Vitamin D, Night Owl for 10 feeds between midnight and 5am, and Perfect Day. Stars mark the higher tiers of a badge. Open them from the profile menu to see progress. Streaks aren’t broken by a day that isn’t over yet. Fun center badges count the games played on this phone by whoever is signed in.

    🎓

    Learning center

    In the profile menu. High-contrast cards are five bold black-and-white designs (Bullseye, Checkerboard, Zigzag, Spiral, and Smile) for calm, awake moments with your baby. Tap one to show it full screen, then swipe or tap the sides to change cards. Invert swaps black and white, and Auto changes cards every 10 seconds. Hold the phone about 8 to 12 inches (20 to 30 cm) away, and stop when your baby looks away or fusses. The screen stays on while a card is showing.

    🎮

    Fun center

    In the profile menu: Bottle Catch, a two-minute, one-thumb game. Drag anywhere to move the basket. Bottles are +10, golden bottles +50, and catching in a row builds up to ×4. Safety pins cost a heart (you have 3), and the pacifier slows everything for 5 seconds. It pauses by itself if you switch away or the feed alarm rings. Sound is off to start; Buzz and Sound are on the start screen. Your first posted score asks for 3 initials, which you can change on the board. High scores has three boards: Everyone (each person’s best across this Cradle), Our family, and My best. Only initials and scores are shown. Scores from rounds played offline post when you’re back online.

    Daily Word: a new word every day at midnight. Guess it in 6 tries. Green is the right letter in the right spot, yellow is in the word but in the wrong spot, and gray isn’t in the word. Pick 4, 5, or 6 letters with the ? button; everyone who picks the same length gets the same word. Hard mode makes you use the letters you’ve found, for 10 extra points. A word is worth 70 points in 1 guess, down to 20 in 6. Your guesses are saved, so you can stop mid-word. Share copies your colored squares, and Practice gives unlimited words with no points. Its high scores show total points with 🔥 streaks, and your family’s grids for today (colors only) once you’ve played.

    Stork Flight: tap anywhere to flap, and fly a stork carrying a bundle through the gaps between the clouds and the chimneys. Each gap is +10, stars are +5, and passing a house with a lit window is a delivery, +25. The stork waits for your first tap, the flight starts gentle and speeds up, and one bump ends it. It pauses by itself if you switch away or the feed alarm rings. Its high scores work like Bottle Catch’s. The buttons at the top of High scores switch between the three games.

    👤

    Profile menu

    The round button at the top right holds Themes (Auto, Light, or Dark, plus fun themes: Dalmatian, Halloween, Thanksgiving, and Christmas), Achievements, Learning center, Fun center, Weights, Settings, Data management (export, import, print, erase all data, and sign out and remove from this phone), this Guide, About, Family, Change password, and Sign out. Settings is where you set the baby’s name, birth date, birth weight, Vitamin D start date, what you track (breast, bottle, pump), feed interval, alarm, notifications, and goals, like the bottle target per feed. The moon button next to it opens Calm.

    This tracker is for keeping records only. Volumes, goals, and supplements should follow your pediatrician or lactation consultant. If you're worried about feeding, diapers, or weight gain, call your baby's doctor.

    About Cradle

    Cradle v1.21
    A baby log that works offline and syncs between family phones through your own Supabase project. No external libraries.

    Not medical advice. Cradle is a notebook. Goals in the app are placeholders, so follow your pediatrician or care team, and call them if you’re worried about your baby.

    Built with AI. Cradle was built with help from Claude, an AI model by Anthropic. It has been tested but not independently reviewed, so keep backups with Export data (in Data management).
